What I’d change and why

A + S-A. Together they remove eleven drawn containers from one screen and leave the two things that were already carrying the hierarchy — the pomegranate/pistachio tone split, and the hairline. That also fixes something the pills were quietly costing you: the hero card and the specialty list looked like the same kind of object, because both were pills on a hairline. With A + S-A the hero keeps the only filled surface on the screen and the specialties are plainly secondary, which is the whole point of the hero/specialty distinction.

Where pills should stay: the location chip in the app bar (it’s a floating status element with nothing around it to anchor to, so it needs an edge) and the buttons (a capsule button is an actual iOS convention, not a default). Removing those too would be over-correcting.
