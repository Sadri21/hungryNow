Location is turned off

HungryNow finds food near you, so it needs location access to work at all. Turning it back on takes two taps.

1 Open Settings → HungryNow

2 Tap Location → While Using the App

Open Settings

One button on purpose. A “Not now” would return to a Home screen whose only button cannot work — there is nothing else in the app to do without location.
